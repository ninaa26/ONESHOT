The physics, pulled apart

Four models were rewritten this cycle: the sail now weather-vanes against a geometric sheet stop, lift ramps out smoothly through the luff, hull drag is derived from hull geometry instead of hand-tuned coefficients, and the rudder sees the true local inflow behind a rate-limited servo. Every figure below runs those models live in your browser — same equations, same NeuralFoil polars as the simulator.

00 · Architecture

One state vector, four force producers

A step is always the same shape: the transform tree places every component, each model returns a force in its own frame, the hub rotates them into the boat frame, sums moments about the CG, and RK4 integrates. Changing a model means changing one box.

02 · updated b522bcd · better sheeting and luffing

The sheet is a stop, not a command

The controller used to dictate a rigid sail angle, which let the sail sit at physically impossible angles to the flow. Now the boom free-spins to the apparent wind angle and the sheet only limits how far out it can swing — exactly like a real mainsheet.

Boom angle vs apparent wind angle

resolved boom angle free (weather-vaning) sail sheet limit

The rule


              

                sailbench/sim/sailboat_hub.py · _resolve_sail_angle_from_sheet
              

              
    
                
  • Below the stop the sail is a weather vane: boom angle tracks |AWA| and the sail makes almost no lift.
    • 
                
  • At the stop the sheet loads up and the sail starts working as a wing.
    • 
                
  • Side follows the sign of the apparent wind in the boat frame, so tacks and gybes flip the boom on their own.
    • 
                
  • Hysteresis: dead-centre apparent wind reuses the previous sail side instead of flickering.
    • 
              

              

                Why it matters for RL: the action is now a sheet limit in
                [0, 90°], a monotone "power" knob. The agent can no longer request a
                sail angle the wind would never allow.

Luffing, ramped instead of switched

            

              A sail near the centreline of the flow flaps and carries no useful lift. The previous
              hybrid model cut lift to zero at a hard 5° threshold; the basic sail now fades lift
              linearly across luff_ramp_deg, so the force never jumps.

Hull drag from hull geometry

            

              BasicHullModel replaced six hand-tuned damping constants with three
              coefficients built from length, beam and draft. Change the boat's dimensions and the
              drag changes with it — no re-tuning pass.

The rudder sees its own inflow

            

              A rudder 0.3 m behind the CG does not feel the boat's velocity — it feels the velocity
              at the blade, which includes the yaw rate. That single term is what makes the
              boat settle out of a turn instead of spinning up.

Helm feel: deadband, rate limit, self-centring

            

              Commands do not snap to the blade. Small inputs are swallowed by a deadband, motion is
              rate-limited like a real servo, and letting go returns the rudder to centre on an
              exponential — which is why manual sailing stopped feeling twitchy.

07 · Foundations

            
Keel: leeway is the angle of attack

            

              The keel is fixed on the centreline, so its angle of attack is just the boat's leeway
              angle atan2(v, u). Side-slip a fraction of a degree and it produces
              hundreds of newtons — that is what lets a sailboat point upwind at all.
            

          

          

            

              
Keel force vs leeway angle

              

              

              

                side force Fy
                induced drag Fx
                lift/drag ratio
              

            

            

              
Numbers at the cursor

              

              

                Watch the stall. Past roughly 10° of leeway the section stalls, side force
                collapses and drag explodes. The sim reaches that in a badly executed tack — the
                boat stops, slides sideways, and has no keel left to bite with.
              

            

          

        


        
        

          

            
08 · Foundations

            
Every foil is a real NACA 0012

            

              Sail, keel and rudder all call Foil.cl_cd, which asks
              NeuralFoil for the section's
              coefficients at the component's Reynolds number. These are the exact curves the
              simulator evaluates, sampled straight out of the Python model.
            

          

          

            

              
NACA0012 polars

              

              

            

            

              
At the cursor

              

              

                Far past stall the model is an extrapolation. The configs allow
                alpha_min/max = ±179°, so downwind the sail is evaluated at angles no
                section database covers. It behaves plausibly — big drag, little lift — but treat
                deep-downwind numbers as qualitative.

11 · Trust

            
Is this page actually the simulator?

            

              The figures above run a JavaScript port of the Python models. To prove the port is
              faithful, the generator recorded per-component forces for
              — randomised states straight out of
              SailboatHub._forces. The browser recomputes all of them on load and
              reports the residual.
            

          

          

            

              
Residualsrunning…

              

              

                Python force magnitude (x) against the browser's, per component. Perfect agreement
                is the diagonal.
              

            

            

              
Per component

              

              

                The browser interpolates the sampled CL/CD table where the Python core calls
                NeuralFoil directly, so a residual of a few thousandths of a percent is the grid.
